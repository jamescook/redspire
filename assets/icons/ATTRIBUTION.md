# Icon attribution

Icons in this folder are sourced from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org),
not from either game's own artwork -- this repo ships no copyrighted Battlespire/Redguard
box art, logos, or in-game assets. They are placeholders for the game-mode picker
(`GameMode.swift`), tracked in `battlespire-macos-ao9.5`.

Steam's and GOG's own logos are **not** included here on purpose: both are registered
trademarks with usage guidelines that require explicit approval for non-affiliated,
non-partner projects (Steam: [partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/branding](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/branding);
GOG: logo ownership stated at [support.gog.com legal info](https://support.gog.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024977313-Legal-info)).
This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve or GOG sp. z o.o., so generic
public-domain icons are used instead to represent "install from Steam" / "install from GOG"
without implying any such affiliation.
